# Art and sound

Every asset in `public/` is CC0 from [Kenney](https://kenney.nl). No credit is
required.

- Shooting Gallery: the atlases in `public/atlas/` (ducks, stall, HUD).
- Kenney Fonts: `public/fonts/kenney-future.ttf`.
- Impact Sounds: `public/sfx/splat.ogg` (impactSoft_heavy_000) and
  `public/sfx/plank.ogg` (impactPlank_medium_000).

`src/audio.ts` synthesizes the music and the other effects.
